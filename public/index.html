Engineering journey

Research. Pilot. Scale-up. Mass production.

Laser platforms. Glovebox cells. Pilot equipment. Hundreds of modules and tens of thousands of cells per day.

Aug 2019–Jul 2021 · Lab scalePurdue · Laser additive manufacturing

Research

At Purdue, laser welding additive-manufacturing research began with laser parameters, experimental platforms, DOE, and process repeatability—the manufacturing-process foundation for everything that followed.

Nov 2021–Apr 2022 · Cell scaleSakuu · Electrolyte R&D

Battery R&D

My battery work began at Sakuu: hand-building small cells in a glovebox while developing electrolytes for solid-state and lithium-metal prototypes.

Apr 2022–Dec 2025 · Pilot scaleLyten · Electrolyte & Li-metal anode R&D

Pilot & Scale-up

At Lyten, electrolyte and lithium-metal anode R&D moved onto pilot equipment, then through scale-up toward large-scale anode manufacturing. Throughput rose 100× and defects fell more than 40%. The work contributed to three patents and three Lyten-era publications.

Dec 2025–Present · Production scaleTesla · LFP mass production

Mass Production

At Tesla, I moved into high-volume production, owning large-scale module manufacturing and the prismatic LFP cell-assembly process: hundreds of modules and tens of thousands of cells per day. Seven stations commissioned and ramped, cycle time down 15%, rolled yield up 10%, Cpk above 1.67.
